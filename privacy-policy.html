Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EXAMRESIZER handles information when you use this website and its browser-based tools. It describes local document processing, browser storage, recruitment network requests, cookies, advertising, analytics, third-party services, and the choices available to visitors.

Scope and responsible operator

This policy applies to https://www.examresizer.me/, its tool views, static policy pages, and current in-browser features. “We” refers to EXAMRESIZER. “User” or “you” refers to a visitor using the website. Different privacy practices may apply on a third-party website or application when you leave this site or use an official exam portal.

Data handled by the document tools

Exam Resizer Pro uses local browser APIs to read an image selected by the user, display a preview, crop or resize it, and create a downloadable output. Our PDF Fast Studio similarly prepares compression, merge, image-to-A4-PDF, page-to-JPG, or page-extraction operations in browser memory. These tools are designed so the selected photo or PDF is not transmitted to an EXAMRESIZER document-processing server.

Browser-local principle: Closing or refreshing the page may clear in-memory images or PDF objects. Downloaded files remain in the location selected by your browser or operating system until you delete or remove them.

Local browser storage and profile data

The CBT interface may store a candidate name or nickname and practice progress using browser local storage or related device storage. This profile is intended to preserve stage status on the same browser and is not a government candidate profile. The name field should not be treated as identity verification and should not contain sensitive numbers or credentials.

Service worker data may support offline application assets or caching. Browsers also maintain download history and ordinary cache data. We do not control third-party browser features that may copy, synchronise, or back up local browser storage under your account settings.

Cookies and local identifiers

A cookie is a small identifier placed or read by a browser. EXAMRESIZER may use local storage and standard web technologies for interface state, PWA installation, downloaded assets, and cached resources. Third-party vendors may set or read cookies to provide advertising, fraud-prevention, measurement, or consent features.

Our pages load Google AdSense/advertising script technology and may contain advertising units. Google and its partners use cookies or similar identifiers for advertising and related measurement. Google may use prior visits to this or other websites to personalise or show ads where applicable and permitted. You can learn about Google's advertising choices at Google Ads & Cookies and manage personalised advertising through Google's Ad Settings.

Analytics and network requests

The website may use browser, hosting, search-console, advertising, or aggregate usage signals to understand which pages and features fail or perform poorly. We do not place a candidate's selected photo or PDF into an analytics event. Recruitment tools request public or registry endpoints to show records and may expose standard server metadata such as IP address, request time, browser type, or requested page as part of normal HTTP operation.

Any cached recruitment record is not guaranteed to be current. When network verification fails, the interface may display a warning or cached verified information. Users must recheck official portals before acting.

Third-party links and embedded viewers

PYQ cards may open PDFs stored with a third-party archive provider or Supabase storage. Official job alerts may link to public government portals or notice documents. Ads, fonts, CDNs, maps, or embedded documents may connect to external providers according to their own policies.

EXAMRESIZER cannot control third-party scripts or pages after you follow a link. Information submitted by you on an official recruitment, payment, exam, or government website is governed by that organisation's policy and application terms.

User choices and controls

  • Use browser privacy mode or clear site data to remove local EXAMRESIZER profiles and cache where supported.
  • Use browser settings to block or delete cookies. Blocking third-party cookies may affect advertising or consent controls.
  • Use Google Ads Settings or industry opt-out tools to manage personalised advertising where available in your region.
  • Do not select a document on a shared or public device if you do not intend to retain or remove the downloaded copy.
  • Do not place Aadhaar details, passwords, OTPs, account credentials, or sensitive payment information in support emails or public feedback.

Data retention

Document selections are intended to exist only in browser memory or in files downloaded by the user. Local mock progress is retained until removed from browser storage or the user clears site data. Downloaded originals and outputs remain under the operating system's normal retention and deletion controls.

Support email and correction reports may be retained as needed to resolve the communication, document a correction, and prevent repeated abuse. A privacy or deletion request should identify the relevant content and explain why deletion is requested.

Children and sensitive data

The website is intended for general educational and recruitment audiences. Candidates should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive identity or medical data into browser fields or support messages. The site is not designed to accept application credentials or final government submissions.

Security limitations

We use reasonable development practices, but no browser, network, or local device can guarantee absolute security. A compromised device, browser extension, public shared computer, or unsafe official link can expose data regardless of the website's local-processing design. Users should keep devices patched, verify domains, and avoid public Wi-Fi for application submissions.

Policy updates

This policy may be revised when features, providers, storage behavior, advertising implementation, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date indicates the latest revision. Continued use after an updated policy is published means you reviewed the current version.

2. Browser-local photo and signature processing

After a candidate chooses an image, the resizer reads the file into browser memory and uses canvas or image APIs for preview and output. File pixels are not sent to an EXAMRESIZER server by the resizer for conversion. The device's browser may keep a temporary blob, cache, or download as normal operating-system behavior. Closing the current page normally releases the active in-memory object, but it does not erase a file already saved in Downloads or a gallery.

Candidate photographs and signatures can reveal identity or biometric information. Users should avoid scanning identity records unnecessarily and should process such images only on a trustworthy device. A shared-device browser can retain previews, download history, clipboard copies, or browser-sync records even where the application itself does not upload the file.

3. Browser-local PDF processing

The PDF suite uses client-side libraries for compression, merging, A4 image conversion, page rendering, and splitting. It is designed to avoid sending selected certificates or memos to an EXAMRESIZER conversion endpoint. PDF.js or pdf-lib data may remain in browser memory, and a generated download may remain in the browser's download folder.

A PDF can contain hidden metadata, embedded images, comments, links, or document text. The browser tools may preserve source content or metadata depending on the operation. A candidate who submits a sensitive document should verify the exported output and remove the original or exported copy from a shared device.

4. Local storage, session state, and service workers

Local storage can retain CBT candidate names or nicknames, stage progress, selected domains, and browser-specific preferences. Session storage or in-memory variables may hold state only for the current page or tab. The PWA service worker can cache application resources for loading or update purposes.

These storage methods are not an official identity system. They do not prove that a named candidate completed an exam or passed an authority's threshold. Browser synchronization, device backup, or developer tools may expose or transfer storage under the settings of a separate provider; EXAMRESIZER does not control those features.

5. Cookies and advertising identifiers

A cookie or identifier can be stored by the website or by an advertising provider. EXAMRESIZER may display Google AdSense units, and Google or its partners may use cookies or local identifiers for ad delivery, frequency, fraud prevention, consent, and aggregate reporting. Personalized advertising depends on regional consent requirements and browser/provider settings.

Cookie controls differ by browser and region. Blocking a third-party cookie may not block all web requests and may affect ad preference controls. Clearing cookies does not necessarily remove local storage or downloaded files. Consent tools or opt-out choices, where shown, apply according to their provider and region.

6. Analytics and technical logs

Hosting providers and network services can process standard request details for availability, security, caching, and troubleshooting. An analytics or advertising product may aggregate page visits or feature interaction, but we do not intentionally send the bytes of a selected photo or PDF through an analytics event. Browser extensions or third-party scripts may add their own behavior outside our direct control.

Recruitment queries may send a filter or public registry request. The source can process the requested category, date, and request metadata according to its own policy. Archive iframe viewers and job notice links create interactions with those external providers or official hosts.

7. External links and official portals

Leaving ExamResizer for a notice, government portal, storage provider, answer-key channel, or advertising destination starts a separate privacy relationship. We cannot control whether that external site asks for an account, location, device identifier, bank detail, or application information. Before entering sensitive data, verify the domain and read that site's policy and terms.

We do not automatically receive an application status or result when a user follows an official link. We cannot access a candidate's government account or guarantee that the linked portal is secure, correct, or current.

8. Support information and user rights

A support email may include an email address, browser details, report text, source URL, and redacted screenshot. For a correction or privacy request, provide only the content needed to locate or remove the affected information. Do not send passwords, OTPs, Aadhaar/PAN numbers, bank credentials, or complete application forms.

Subject to applicable law, a visitor may ask to access, correct, delete, or restrict an existing support record or explain an advertising concern. We may need to verify the request or retain a limited record to prevent fraud and complete the correction. We can limit or decline a deletion request when it conflicts with legal, security, archive, or correction obligations.

9. Practical user choices

  1. Use a private browser profile or screen lock for confidential documents.
  2. Clear downloads and site storage after using a shared device.
  3. Block or manage third-party cookies only when you understand the possible effect on consent and advertising.
  4. Keep browser and PDF software updated.
  5. Verify HTTPS and the organisation name before entering data on an external portal.
  6. Use the least data necessary in screenshots and support emails.

10. Data retention and deletion

In-memory document state is released when the page or browser context is closed or refreshed, subject to browser behavior and already downloaded files. Local CBT data remains until the browser profile is cleared or the feature provides a reset. Cache and download history follow browser settings and provider controls.

Support reports are retained only for the length reasonably needed to investigate, document, and prevent repeated misuse. Advertising and registry providers control their own retention periods. A request to delete a support record should include the request date, email address, exact topic, and no unnecessary credentials.

11. Security and shared devices

Browser-local processing reduces some server exposure but does not make a device safe. Browser extensions, malware, account synchronisation, remote access, public printing, or disk recovery can retain or expose selected files. Users should avoid public devices for application documents and log out of account-linked browser synchronization where appropriate.

Frequently asked privacy questions

Are my photo and PDF uploaded?

The resizer and PDF tools are designed to process them in browser memory. External hosting, browser sync, and embedded viewing may involve separate network behavior, which is described by the provider and this policy.

What should I do on a shared computer?

Use a private window if appropriate, remove downloads, clear EXAMRESIZER site data, close the browser, and sign out of synchronized accounts. Do not leave a candidate photograph or certificate open in a tab or download folder.

Does declining cookies stop all advertising?

No consent or ad preferences may change personalized advertising, but non-personalized or non-consented ad treatment can still depend on provider rules and jurisdiction. Browser cookie blocking can also affect site and provider behavior.

Can I delete my CBT progress?

Clear the browser's local storage/site data or remove the candidate profile using available interface controls. Clearing storage on one browser does not remove data stored by another device or browser.

How do I ask a privacy question?

Email chandu52311@gmail.com with the page URL and a short description. Do not include document or account credentials. You may also use Contact Us for category guidance.
