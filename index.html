Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are uploaded photos stored on your server?
No. All resizing and compression happens 100% locally on your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your sensitive documents never leave your phone or computer.
Why does the portal reject resized files?
Government portals strictly validate both aspect ratios and exact file weights. A photo that is 50.1 KB will trigger instant rejection. Our smart binary compression ensures it stays strictly within bounds.
