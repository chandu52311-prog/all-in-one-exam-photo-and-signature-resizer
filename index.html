Step-by-Step Usage & Troubleshooting Guide

How to Resize Your Documents Correctly:

  1. Select your target recruitment commission or public sector examination from the top buttons.
  2. Choose whether you want to format a Passport Photo or a Signature.
  3. Click on the dotted upload container to pick your source image file from your device.
  4. Tap the maroon GENERATE button. The browser engine processes the exact pixel and byte dimensions.
  5. Click Download Resized File to save the final image to your local device storage.

Troubleshooting Compression Bounds:

  • Output Below Permissible Minimum KB: Very low-resolution or heavily pre-compressed images can end up under-size. Always capture your photograph in sharp focus with high resolution.
  • Output Exceeding Maximum Limit: Avoid cluttered backgrounds. Plain white or light grey backgrounds compress cleanly without exceeding portal thresholds.
  • Signature Clarity Disqualification: Always sign using a dark black or deep blue ballpoint pen on plain white unruled paper without wrinkles or shadows.

Comprehensive Guide: Image Formatting & Portal Compliance

Automated verification gateways at central commissions and state power utilities reject application submissions that fail exact byte boundaries or dimension aspect ratios.

1. The 80% Facial Coverage Rule

Prominent recruitment bodies mandate that the applicant's face must encompass 70% to 80% of the photograph. Ensure straight eye contact with both ears clearly visible. Head coverings, tinted lenses, or dark spectacles are strictly restricted unless for religious attire.

2. Background Tone and Illumination

Always maintain a clean, uniform, white or light grey backdrop. Avoid flash reflections, dark cast shadows, or casual background locations to prevent automated document scrutiny failures.

3. Client-Side Browser Security Architecture

Our resizer operates purely client-side through the HTML5 Canvas API. All compression and scaling procedures execute in your local device memory without uploading images to any remote server.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why do exam portals show "File size out of range"?
Server gateways check file headers strictly. If a portal requires 20 KB to 50 KB, uploading 19.9 KB or 50.1 KB will trigger an automated upload block. Our engine uses binary search compression to fit files safely within the bounds.
Will reducing KB reduce print quality on my Admit Card?
No. Our engine maintains standard proportional aspect ratios using high-quality bicubic canvas interpolation so that your hall ticket photo remains crisp and clear.
