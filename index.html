Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are uploaded photos stored on your server?
No. All resizing and compression happens 100% locally on your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your sensitive documents never leave your phone or computer.
What is the difference between State PSC and PSUs?
State PSCs (like APPSC and TGPSC) conduct administrative Group 1, 2, and 4 services. PSUs (like APTRANSCO, TSGENCO, and PGCIL) are government-owned power and industrial corporations with specific technical recruitment requirements.
