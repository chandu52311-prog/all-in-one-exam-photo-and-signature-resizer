About Exam Resizer Pro

Exam Resizer Pro was built to solve a persistent technical challenge faced by Indian engineering and civil service job aspirants: sudden online portal rejections due to strict file size (KB) and aspect ratio criteria.

Many students spend hours visiting internet cafes or using heavy photo editing suites. Our mission is to provide an instantaneous, 100% offline, privacy-first tool that converts passport photos and signatures to match the specifications of SSC, UPSC, GATE, State PSCs, and Power Utilities (TRANSCO/GENCO) in a single tap.
