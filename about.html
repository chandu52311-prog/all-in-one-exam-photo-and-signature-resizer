About EXAMRESIZER

ExamResizer.me is an independent educational and document-preparation utility created to help competitive-examination candidates organise photographs, signatures, PDFs, previous-year papers, calculation practice, mock tests, and recruitment information. It focuses on practical, browser-based tasks that can otherwise require separate editors, internet cafes, or repeated manual conversion.

What we provide

The platform brings six candidate-facing tools into one consistent workspace. Exam Resizer Pro helps apply selected image dimensions and file-size targets. Speed Aptitude Arena provides timed calculation and English drills. The PYQ Hub organises historical papers for study and review. The CBT Exam Terminal offers staged mock practice with saved browser progress. PDF Fast Studio works with common certificate and application-document operations. Government Job Alerts presents recruitment records with official-link and status safeguards.

Our purpose

We aim to remove avoidable friction from preparation. A candidate should not have to guess whether a photograph is in the correct range, understand three separate PDF tools, or manually organise a useful previous-year-paper review. Our interfaces are intended to explain the task, expose the current action clearly, and preserve useful original controls where the user needs to make a final choice.

Educational utility does not mean official authority. EXAMRESIZER does not publish, replace, or approve a notification. It does not determine rank, admission, eligibility, vacancy, or document acceptance. Those decisions belong to the relevant examination body and application portal.

Privacy-first design

Photo and PDF operations are designed to run inside the visitor's browser. The resizer and PDF tools do not require images or application PDFs to be transmitted to a remote document-processing endpoint. Browser-based mock progress and name settings may use local storage on the device; users may remove that data using browser controls or the interface's profile option.

Recruitment and archive information depends on network services and may be unavailable, cached, corrected, or updated. We describe those states honestly rather than presenting stale or unverified results as current.

Editorial and update approach

Examination specifications can change after a notification, corrigendum, or portal update. We therefore separate general guidance from current-cycle confirmation. The site should help users identify what to verify, but users must compare the downloaded file or preparation decision with the latest official instructions.

Corrections are important. If a tool label is unclear, a specification appears outdated, an archive link is wrong, or a recruitment notice is misclassified, the editorial team can review the exact post, year, set, or URL. Please include enough context for a targeted correction.

How to report a correction

Send the tool name, what is wrong, the official notice or source URL, and a screenshot or filename only if it contains no confidential identity or payment information. Do not email Aadhaar, OTP, passwords, bank details, or complete application credentials.

Frequently asked questions

Is EXAMRESIZER an SSC, UPSC, RRB, GATE, state government, or PSU website?

No. It is an independent educational utility. Exam names and acronyms are used only to describe candidate tools and content; they do not imply government ownership or endorsement.

Can a generated image or PDF be guaranteed to be accepted?

No. Acceptance depends on the active official notice, the application portal, file properties, and the quality of the source. The preview and final upload should be checked separately.

Does EXAMRESIZER store submitted documents?

Photo and signature processing is browser-local. PDF tools are also designed to process in the browser. Uploaded data should not be described as server-saved by these tools. Network and advertising services are explained in the Privacy Policy.

Editorial mission for Indian examination aspirants

Indian candidates frequently move between a conducting authority notice, an application portal, a separate image editor, a PDF scanner, and a study resource. The resulting friction is especially difficult for a first-time applicant: a valid photograph can be rejected because of an unexpected KB limit, a useful previous-year paper can be copied from the wrong shift, or an apparently active vacancy notice can be several months out of date.

Our editorial mission is therefore practical. Each EXAMRESIZER guide should explain what action is available, what information the user must verify, and what failure can occur. We prefer clear preparation steps and labelled examples to promotional language. We do not describe a recommendation from a participating authority unless the official source supports it. Where a requirement varies, we state the variation instead of presenting one preset as universally valid.

The mission also includes access for candidates with limited equipment. Browser-local tools can reduce dependence on specialised editing software and avoid sending an image to a separate conversion service. This does not mean every user has equal internet access or device power. Interface descriptions remain concise, buttons have text labels, and large conversion tasks are kept as separate actions so users can understand which stage is active.

How the six tools work safely

Exam Resizer Pro

The resizer reads a selected image through browser APIs, creates a preview, applies the chosen dimensions or crop workflow, and prepares a downloadable file. The active examination preset is a convenience aid. It does not alter an official notice or decide whether a portal accepts the generated JPEG or PNG. Candidates should compare the result with the notice and the portal preview before submission.

Speed Aptitude & Calculation Arena

The arena creates short timed calculation or English sets in the browser. It tracks streaks, accuracy, and progression for the active session. No school, college, or recruiting authority receives a candidate’s arena record. Practice data is intended for self-evaluation and should not be interpreted as a psychometric or admission result.

PYQ Hub

The PYQ Hub organises catalog entries by authority and stream. When a sector or year is chosen, the interface presents available archive cards and an embedded or downloadable source. Paper availability can differ because of storage, licensing, source quality, or a file still being corrected. An archive card is not a substitute for the conducting authority’s current key or syllabus.

CBT Exam Terminal

The CBT terminal presents staged mock questions, timer, palette, response controls, marking, and result review. It may store a name and progress in the browser. The candidate should close or clear data on a shared device. Any +2.00/-0.50 or 35% threshold used in practice must be checked against the real examination rules.

PDF Mega Suite

PDF operations use browser libraries to compress, merge, convert, render, or split local files. The module does not upload a certificate to an EXAMRESIZER document server. Because output quality depends on the source and browser, candidates must inspect every downloaded result, preserve the original, and follow the official portal’s page-count and file-limit rules.

Government Job Alerts

The alert module requests verified records and applies category and closing-date filters. Network failure can leave a warning or cached record visible. No cached status is a guarantee that an application is open. The last editorial step is always to open the official gazette or application portal.

PYQ archive curation standards for 2020–2026

Our PYQ catalogue is organised around authority, post or discipline, year, shift/session, and source availability. A curation pass should verify that the visible title, selected stream, year, session, and stored file refer to the same examination. We avoid describing a PDF as final or official merely because it is hosted in an archive; available metadata and the source itself must agree.

Quality and accuracy verification process

Before publishing a correction, we favour a source-to-interface review rather than a social-media claim. The review collects the affected URL or page, the official PDF or notice, the exact field at issue, and the expected text or behaviour. For a dimension change, we compare the notice, application portal, and any portal validation information. For a recruitment alert, we compare notification number, category, closing date, official link, and validity status.

Corrections are not assumed to be permanent. A notification amendment or portal update can require a second review. We aim to leave a stale or uncertain record unavailable or labelled as verification pending where the interface supports that state, rather than silently replacing it with an assumption. Users should still report a discrepancy and preserve the official URL for reference.

Accessibility and free access commitment

EXAMRESIZER uses browser controls, text labels, responsive layouts, and direct navigation where possible so the most common tools remain usable from mobile, tablet, and desktop devices. Keyboard-readable buttons and visible state changes are part of the intended experience. Detailed frequently asked questions

How often are image specifications updated?

There is no fixed universal update schedule. A specification should be rechecked whenever a new notification, corrigendum, post, or portal is opened. Candidate feedback can help identify a mismatch, but official text remains the final source.

How are recruitment records ranked or filtered?

Available filters represent category, authority type, or application status selected in the interface. Verified records are filtered for active dates and safe official links, subject to registry availability and the module's technical validation.

Can I use a PYQ to predict next year's paper?

Past papers are useful for patterns, topic coverage, command words, and timing practice. They cannot predict exact questions or replace the current syllabus and exam notice.

Where is my candidate name or test progress stored?

The CBT profile can use local browser storage on the same device and browser. It is not an official candidate registration. Clear site data or use the profile controls on a shared device.

How can I request removal or correction of a source link?

Use the Contact page or email chandu52311@gmail.com. Include the exact URL, affected file or card, ownership or correction basis, and, for copyright claims, enough detail to identify the relevant work.
