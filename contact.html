Before you report a problem

Refresh the page once, confirm whether the browser and network are stable, and retry the action. For image or PDF problems, note the source file type and a non-sensitive output size. Do not send the original document if it contains confidential identity or account information.

For exam-format problems, include the examination name, post, notification number, official notice URL, requested dimension/KB rule, and where the rule appears. A link to the official notice is more useful than an edited image or forwarded message.

What to include in an email

  • Subject line beginning with the category, such as “Technical – PDF merge” or “PYQ correction – RRB JE 2022.”
  • A short reproduction: where you opened the tool, what you selected, and what happened.
  • The official notice or source for a correction.
  • Your browser and device type, especially if the issue is limited to mobile or Safari/Chrome.
  • The least amount of personal data needed; redact names and document numbers.

Response expectations

A submission is not confirmation that every suggested change is appropriate or that an official rule has changed.

Frequently asked questions

Can you fix a file rejected by an application portal?

We can help diagnose the selected format if you provide the official notice and the portal's public error message. Acceptance still depends on the conducting authority and live portal validation.

Can you confirm my eligibility or exam cutoff?

No. Eligibility, cutoffs, vacancy numbers, and selection status must come from the official notification and authority record.

Can you send a direct answer key or application link?

If you report a missing or incorrect source, we may review and update archive or alert links. We cannot replace the official conducting body's result or answer-key channel.

Reporting a PYQ or answer-key discrepancy

A useful PYQ report identifies both the interface record and the source. Include the authority, exam name, post or discipline, year, shift or session, set, question number, and exact mismatch. For an answer-key concern, include the official final or provisional key URL, the set, and the official question or response reference. A screenshot can help, but it should exclude personal identity details and should show enough of the header to identify the paper.

What to do before reporting

  1. Confirm that the archive card and opened PDF use the same year and set.
  2. Compare the question number with the official response sheet rather than only a social-media solution.
  3. Check whether the official key has a correction, challenge, or revised response notice.
  4. Record the source URL and whether the issue is a wrong title, broken PDF, missing page, or disputed answer.

Do not send a fabricated or personally altered answer key and do not present an unofficial solution as final evidence. If the report concerns a legal objection or significant ownership dispute, explain the basis clearly and allow the exact source documents to control the review.

Reporting photo or signature specification updates

Application notices may update dimensions, file type, KB range, background, DPI, signature size, or crop rules. To report a mismatch, send the full examination name and post, notification or circular number, official URL, and a quotation or screenshot of the relevant line. State whether the discrepancy is in the preset label, the output size, the generated dimensions, or the live portal message.

Do not send a candidate's passport photograph or Aadhaar details as evidence. An example image can be created from a generic, non-sensitive test source. We cannot verify every post-specific cycle in real time, so the official notice remains the candidate's first and final check.

Reporting job alert corrections

For a job notice, include the authority, notification number, post code, vacancy category, start date, closing date and time, official gazette/PDF URL, and the application URL. Identify whether the card is wrong, outdated, duplicated, or still showing after the deadline. We also need an official correction or amendment if a withdrawal, postponement, or revised vacancy has changed the notice.

Do not forward a WhatsApp or social-media screenshot as the only source. The report should contain enough official source evidence to distinguish a genuine update from a repost. Never send your application password or payment reference unless a separate official privacy process explicitly requires it.

Copyright and DMCA-style notices

Rights holders can report a page, filename, storage link, or displayed description that should be restricted or reviewed. A notice should identify:

  • The exact URL or archive record involved.
  • The work or document claimed to be protected.
  • The claimant's authority to act and suitable contact details.
  • The requested action, such as removal, correction, or relinking.
  • A good-faith statement that the complaint is accurate and not made merely as a competitive or strategic objection.

A rights claim does not remove the obligation to protect a candidate's lawful, official, or educational interests beyond the scope of the request.

Contact FAQs

How should I redact a screenshot?

Remove names, registration numbers, Aadhaar or PAN details, signatures, payment references, phone numbers, and OTPs. Keep enough information to show the tool error and, for a spec report, the official notice heading or URL.

Can I contact you for application help or form filling?

No. We can clarify a public tool limitation or report a possible website defect, but we cannot enter data, create accounts, make payments, or guarantee application submission.

What if my correction is declined?

A decline or pending status can mean the official evidence is inconsistent or broader verification is needed. Ask for the category of issue and provide the exact notice or source rather than repeatedly resending the same message.

Final checklist before sending your message

Use a clear subject, state the affected page or tool, give one reproducible description, identify the official source, and explain the expected correction. Remove personal documents, account sessions, browser passwords, payment confirmations, and OTP information. If the report includes an image or PDF, attach only a redacted or synthetic test file unless the official source itself is public.

For an advertising inquiry, retain the page URL and ad position. For a copyright notice, provide a direct URL and ownership basis. For a PYQ issue, preserve the year, set, and final official key. For a job-alert issue, include the notification number and closing date. These details allow a reviewer to investigate the exact record instead of guessing.
